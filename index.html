Shortcuts

Mouse
scroll: zoom at cursor · drag: pan (rotate in circular view)
double-click: zoom in (Shift out) · click a feature: zoom to it
Keyboard
/ search · R reset view · V circular / linear · ? this panel · Esc close
+ - zoom in / out 25% · ← → pan 12% (Shift 50%)
↑ ↓ or ] [ Y scale up / down
y autoscale Y to this view and hold it (press again in a new place to refit) · Shift+y let Y follow the view again
s S smooth less / more (all tracks)
b B fewer / more bins (all tracks)
t bars (default) / area outline
Bars only: h fixed height per read (linear)
Area only: p shared Y · k K sharpen less / more
Double-click a sample name in the panel to rename it
